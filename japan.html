Religion

Shinto is a uniquely Japanese indigenous religion deeply integrated into Japanese culture, with a history of approximately 2,500 years. Unlike Christianity or Islam, it has no scriptures or specific teachings, nor does it have a founder. It is one of the world's quite rare religions. This religion is deeply integrated, so even if people are not in Shintoism, their behaviors and spirits get very close to it.

Transportation

Did you now that there are over 30,000km (about 19000 miles) of rails for public Transportation? You don't need to buy a car especially if you are going to urban cities. The system of these transportation systems are very high-tech and you only need a card to use these things. Also, you can use the card for a shopping too.
